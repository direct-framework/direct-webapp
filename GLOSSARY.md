# DIRECT domain

DIRECT describes capabilities used in digital research. Shared framework content
is separate from a person's assessments.

## Framework

**Competency domain**:
A broad thematic grouping of competencies.

**Competency**:
A combination of skills, knowledge, behaviours and professional practices needed
for effective work in a particular context. Each belongs to one competency domain.

**Skill**:
A learnable, observable and assessable ability or behaviour. Each belongs to one
competency; related skills can be linked across the hierarchy.
_Avoid_: Using skill and competency interchangeably.

**Skill level**:
The proficiency, autonomy or awareness demonstrated when applying a skill.
The shared scale is 0: None or N/A, 1: Awareness, 2: Working, 3: Practitioner,
4: Expert. Its descriptions cover technical and non-technical skills.
Level 0 combines no demonstrated ability with not applicable; an unassessed skill
has no selected level and is distinct from zero.

**Tool**:
A computational application, platform or system used when applying a skill.

**Language**:
A formal language for programming or describing and exchanging data.

**Methodology**:
A structured approach or practice for carrying out work.

Tools, languages and methodologies demonstrate skills; they are not themselves
skills.

**Learning resource**:
Material or an activity supporting skill development or learning to use tools,
languages or methodologies. Resources can relate directly to either.

**Provider**:
The organisation or person supplying a learning resource.

## Personal assessment

**Self-assessment**:
A person's assessment of their own level for a skill, separate from the shared
skill definition.

**Skill profile**:
A person's collection of skill assessments.

**Competency wheel**:
A visualisation of a skill profile.
_Alias_: Skill wheel.

## Teams

**Team**:
A group of people who share their skill profiles for collaboration. A team is
visible only to its members.

**Team member**:
A person who has accepted an invitation to a team. Members can see the team, its
aggregate skill profile, the names of the other members and who the managers
are, but not other members' skill profiles. A member may leave
a team at any time, which ends any access others had to their skill profile
through that team.

**Team manager**:
A team member who administers the team. Managers can see every member's whole
skill profile, edit or delete the team, invite and remove members, and promote or
demote managers. A team always has at least one manager; whoever creates a team is its
first manager.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin.

**Team invitation**:
An offer from a team manager for a registered person to join a team. The person
becomes a team member only after accepting it; a pending invitation grants no
access. The invitee may decline it and any manager may withdraw it. A person has
at most one pending invitation per team.

**Aggregate skill profile**:
A combined view of the skill profiles of a team's members. It is only shown when
enough members contribute that no individual's skill profile can be deduced
from it.
